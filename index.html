How the leaderboards work

Power = total squats ÷ days since you started

Your start day counts as day 1, so power stays fair for people who joined late. Today and This week (Monday to Sunday) count squats logged for those days. Streak counts days in a row with squats; it stays alive until the end of today. Most improved compares the last 7 days with the 7 days before.
